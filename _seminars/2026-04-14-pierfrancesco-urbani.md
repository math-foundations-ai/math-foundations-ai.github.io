Using dynamical mean field theory, the talk analysed training in large two-layer neural networks. The results identified a slow timescale associated with growth in Gaussian/Rademacher complexity, an inductive bias towards low-complexity solutions under suitable initialisation, and a separation between feature learning and overfitting. The analysis also found a later “feature unlearning” regime with non-monotonic test error. Joint work with Andrea Montanari.
